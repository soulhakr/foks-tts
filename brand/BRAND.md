# FOKS TTS brand palette

Single source of truth: [`palette.json`](palette.json), generated by [`build_palette.py`](build_palette.py)
(`python3 brand/build_palette.py`). Preview: [`swatches.png`](swatches.png). The app icon
(`app/Icon/make_icon.swift`) reads its colors from `palette.json`, so the two never drift.

## The idea

Rust on forest green (the dark theme) and rust on sand (the light theme) are the same system seen at two
brightness levels: one accent, two interchangeable neutrals.

| Family | Anchor | Hue | Role |
|---|---|---|---|
| **rust** | `rust-500` #DE6226, `rust-600` #B84016 | 20° | the fox and the single accent color |
| **forest** | `forest-600` #24664C, `forest-900` #0A241B | 156° | dark neutral / secondary |
| **sand** | `sand-50` #FFFAF2, `sand-100` #FFF3E4, `sand-300` #EED6BA | 33° | light neutral / secondary |

**Why it is balanced:** rust (20°) has blue-teal as its opposite; forest green (156°) is about 44° from that
opposite, a **split-complementary** pairing that gives strong contrast without a harsh red/green clash.
Sand (33°) is nearly the same hue as rust at low saturation, so it acts as the warm neutral that
ties the two together. Ramps are interpolated in OKLab so steps look evenly spaced.

**Proportion (60-30-10):** about 60% neutral surface (forest in dark mode, sand in light), about 30% the
other neutral or a ramp step for surfaces and borders, and no more than about 10% rust. Rust is for the
fox, the primary button, links, and the word highlight, not for large backgrounds.

## Contrast (WCAG 2.x, computed)

| Theme | Pair | Use | Ratio | AA body text (4.5) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| dark | text on background | body text | 15.77 | yes |
| dark | text-muted on background | secondary text | 11.68 | yes |
| dark | text on surface | text on surface | 6.56 | yes |
| dark | accent on background | accent / links / icons | 4.59 | yes |
| dark | on-accent on accent | button label | 5.13 | yes |
| light | text on background | body text | 17.63 | yes |
| light | text-muted on background | secondary text | 14.52 | yes |
| light | text on surface | text on surface | 16.74 | yes |
| light | accent on background | accent / links / icons | 5.34 | yes |
| light | on-accent on accent | button label | 5.34 | yes |

Rust-500 on forest-900 is the tightest pair (4.59:1): fine for the accent text and UI, but do not put
small body text in rust-500 on dark. Use `text` or `text-muted` for body copy.

## Guardrails (why these colors)

- **Not Firefox / Mozilla.** Firefox's identity is a bright yellow-orange gradient fox on a blue-purple
  globe. Keep the fox a deeper rust/copper, with no yellow glow, and never use blue-violet or purple
  backgrounds. **Not GitLab** either (also an orange fox): stay on forest and sand grounds.
- **No yellow-orange gradients, no purple, no blue-violet** anywhere in the identity.
- The word highlight can stay a conventional yellow for legibility over text; it is a UI affordance, not branding.
- The fox is a plain red fox. We deliberately do not use a white kitsune mask (sacred-adjacent imagery) or
  fake Japanese text.
- This is a comparison against brands we know, not a trademark search; run a real check before a public release.
